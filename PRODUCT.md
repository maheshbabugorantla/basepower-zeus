# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Two audiences, weighted equally. The first is the room at a gate demo: hackathon judges and Base Power people watching a laptop on a projector for five minutes, who need to read the ranking and believe the numbers from across the room. The second is a Base growth or territory analyst at a desk, using the tool for longer sessions to decide which Texas neighborhoods and homes Base should approach next, and why. Both are answering the same question; the analyst just stays longer and digs into the evidence.

## Product Purpose

Base Power Zeus ranks single-family homes and block groups inside Base's current Texas service area for outreach, using four real public-data signals: outage exposure, grid value, installability, and household fit. Success is a ranked list a Base analyst would act on, where every number on screen opens the source file it came from, and missing data reads as missing rather than estimated.

## Positioning

Every number is traceable: Zeus shows only real public data, links each value to its downloaded source file, and says "not loaded" instead of guessing.

## Brand Personality

A trustworthy instrument: calm, precise, evidence-first. Every number should look audited. Base's green is used sparingly, as signal rather than decoration. The voice is plain and exact: units always shown, weights labeled as team choices, and absent data called absent.

## Design Principles

- **Provenance is the interface.** A value without a path to its source file is a bug, not a styling choice. The route from any number to its manifest row is one interaction away.
- **Missing is a state, not a zero.** "Not loaded" and "not available" are first-class, visually distinct states with a reason. They are never an empty cell, a dash, or a 0.
- **Legible from the back of the room, dense at the desk.** Primary answers read on a projector; the evidence underneath rewards a close look.
- **Choices are labeled as choices.** Scoring weights and channel recommendations are the team's parameters and look like controls, never like data.
- **Show the result, whatever it says.** Backtests and validation numbers get neutral treatment. There is no green for good or red for bad on our own model's report card.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. Map, chart, and signal colors must stay distinguishable for color-blind viewers (deuteranopia, protanopia, tritanopia), and no meaning is carried by color alone. Honor `prefers-reduced-motion`.
