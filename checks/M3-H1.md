## How each county/utility -> load zone mapping was determined (not guessed)

- **Austin Energy -> LZ_AEN (Travis County):** ERCOT's official Load Zone
  map (https://www.ercot.com/files/assets/2023/06/05/ERCOT-Maps_Load-Zone.jpg,
  linked from https://www.ercot.com/news/mediakit/maps) shades the City of
  Austin service footprint as a separate "Austin Energy (AEN)" load zone,
  distinct from the surrounding "South" zone that covers the rest of
  Travis County. Austin Energy is one of ERCOT's four Non-Opt-In Entity
  (NOIE) Load Zones (Austin, CPS, LCRA, RAYBN).

- **CenterPoint Energy Houston Electric -> LZ_HOUSTON (Harris County):**
  same ERCOT Load Zone map shades essentially all of Harris County as the
  "Houston" competitive load zone; CenterPoint is the TDSP for that area.

- **Pedernales Electric Cooperative and Bluebonnet Electric Cooperative ->
  LZ_LCRA, not LZ_SOUTH (Travis County):** this was the one that required
  real verification rather than a guess. The ERCOT Load Zone map shows
  most of non-Austin-Energy Travis County shaded green ("South"), with
  small yellow "LCRA" patches scattered inside it — so a naive county-level
  read of the map alone could wrongly conclude "LZ_SOUTH" for these co-ops.
  Two ERCOT Board memos resolve this correctly:
  - `10 Load Zone Changes - Request of Lower Colorado River Authority -
    Template v2.pdf` (ERCOT Board, Oct 17 2023,
    https://www.ercot.com/files/docs/2023/10/09/10%20Load%20Zone%20Changes%20-%20Request%20of%20Lower%20Colorado%20River%20Authority%20-%20Template%20v2.pdf)
    quotes Nodal Protocol Section 3.4.3(1)(d) verbatim: "NOIEs may
    participate in only one NOIE Load Zone, and all Loads served by that
    NOIE must be contained within that Load Zone." LCRA's NOIE Load Zone
    is LZ_LCRA.
  - `13.2 Non-Opt-In Entity (NOIE) Load Zone Change - Request of Lamar
    Electric Cooperative - REVISED.pdf` (ERCOT Board, Dec 8-9 2025,
    https://www.ercot.com/files/docs/2025/12/04/13.2-Non-Opt-In-Entity-NOIE-Load-Zone-Changes-Request-of-Lamar-Electric-Cooperative-REVISED.pdf)
    documents the general pattern by precedent: a distribution
    cooperative's load moves into or out of "LCRA NOIE Load Zone" based on
    whether it currently has a wholesale power supply arrangement with
    LCRA (citing Guadalupe Valley Electric Cooperative's 2016 exit from
    the LCRA zone "upon expiration of power supply arrangement with LCRA").
  - LCRA's own site (https://www.lcra.org/energy/electric-power/,
    retrieved 2026-09-25) lists its current wholesale electric cooperative
    customers as exactly four: Bandera, Bluebonnet, Hamilton County, and
    Pedernales. PEC's 2024/2025 rate filings put roughly 65%+ of its power
    supply as still coming from LCRA.
  - Putting the protocol rule and the current customer list together: PEC
    and Bluebonnet load in Travis County is settled in LZ_LCRA today, not
    the geographic LZ_SOUTH zone their territory happens to sit inside on
    the map. The `notes` column in the CSV carries this reasoning per row
    so a future reader isn't misled by the map alone.

## Open items / things NOT resolved here

- The CSV does not attempt a complete parcel-level or sub-county carve-up
  of exactly which Travis County addresses fall under Austin Energy vs.
  PEC vs. Bluebonnet service territory (that's a GIS/parcel join problem
  for a pipeline ticket, not this citation CSV). This ticket only
  establishes utility -> load zone, one row per utility per county.
- LZ_NORTH (Oncor) is verified as a real settlement point but has no CSV
  row, per the "no invented county" reasoning above.
