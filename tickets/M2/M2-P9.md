# M2-P9 — Permit path and permit risk per jurisdiction

## What to build

User request (after Base COO's Utility Dive piece on city-hall permitting): quantify permit risk and show each home's permit path.
Measured already from the manifested Austin permits raw file (applieddate, issue_date, issue_method, status_current, contractor):
battery permits median 7 days (p90 36) before Texas SB 1252 (effective 2025-09-01) vs 12 days (p90 34) after; 100% issued via the
in-person Permit Center; ~9% never finish (6.2% expired, 2.8% withdrawn); Base Power's own 327 Austin permits: 2026Q3 median 14 days,
p90 20. SB 1252 bars cities from regulating residential energy backup systems except municipally owned utilities in their service
area -> Austin Energy territory keeps a city battery permit (Auxiliary Power Electrical Permit); Oncor-area cities follow state rules only.
Build: (1) permit_timelines source reading the already-manifested raw file (no new download) -> core.permit_timelines per permit
(permit_number, label, applied, issued, days, issue_method, status, contractor, source_id) and core.permit_path_stats per
jurisdiction x label x period (median/p90 days, share never finished, share online, n). (2) data/manual/permit_rules.csv with cited
rows: SB 1252 text/effective date (TDLR + capitol.texas.gov), Austin Energy auxiliary power permit requirement (Austin Energy permitting
manual), unincorporated Travis fire code (existing ahj_facts rows) — loaded like retail_market.csv. (3) Per home: permit_path
('city_battery_permit' Austin Energy area | 'state_rules_only' Oncor-area city | 'county_fire_code' unincorporated) from territory +
jurisdiction; permit_risk term = anchored expected days + never-finished share (0-1), a small installability penalty (default weight 2)
wired into the scoring functions and api.home_score_breakdown (backward compatible). (4) Web: plain 'Permit path' line on the home page
with provenance (e.g. "City of Austin (Austin Energy): city battery permit required, typically ~12 days (slowest 10%: 34+), in person");
an Overview panel 'Time to permit a home battery in Austin' by quarter incl. Base Power's own permits. Unknown timings show
MissingState 'not published', never an estimate.

## Acceptance criteria
