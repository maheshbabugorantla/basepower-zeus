# M2-P8 — Home-level signals, outage vs Texas distributors, and top signals that say why a home stands out

## What to build

User feedback: almost every home shows the same top signals. Measured cause: every signal is block-group level (homes in one block group
tie and share chips; page 1 = 50 tied homes), 'Outside flood zone' is 1.0 for 98.5% of homes so it is always #1, and the outage percentile
is 0 for every home (only Austin Energy reports SAIDI in Travis, so ranking it against itself gives 0).
Fix, all from data already loaded with provenance:
(1) Home-level signals in core.mv_home_signals: owner_65 (TCAD over-65 exemption, core.parcels.ov65_exempt), home_solar, home_ev,
home_generator (the home's own labelled Austin permits via core.permit_labels -> permits.tcad_id). Permit flags are known only inside the
City of Austin permit area: outside it they are null with reason 'no permit data for this city', never false. A home whose own permit shows
a battery is flagged 'Already has a home battery (permit <date>)' and does not get a positive battery signal. Scored as 1/0 terms with
their own sliders; block-group signals stay.
(1b) Installability (user request; verified against Base's own help article help.basepowercompany.com/en/articles/10280705: Austin
requires a 150-200A main breaker, 200A with solar or two batteries; one main breaker box, not in a closet, on the meter's wall, meter
<= 6 ft; the article states NO home-age rule). Load year built from IMP_DET.TXT (yr_built of the main living-area improvement) in the
already-manifested TCAD 2026 export zip — new source tcad_improvements reading the same raw zip (reuse tcad_layout.py), core.parcel_improvements with source_id. Add an installability term:
newer home and/or the home's own panel-upgrade permit (permit_labels 'panel', 8,080) = panel more likely to qualify. Plus a visible
filter 'Hide homes built before 2000' labelled as a team choice, not a Base rule, default OFF. Home page shows 'Built <year>' and, for
pre-2000 homes without a panel permit, one plain line: 'Older home: Base needs a 150-200A main breaker in Austin; check the panel.'
with the Base article as provenance.
(2) Outage percentile: rank each distributor's latest SAIDI against all Texas distributors reporting to EIA-861 that year (45 in 2025),
not against homes, so Austin Energy gets a real percentile; say so on the home page ('fewer outage minutes than N% of Texas utilities'). When a distributor reports no SAIDI (e.g. Oncor), use the
county EAGLE-I figure (customer-hours x 60 / customers served = minutes per customer, same unit) as a clearly labelled proxy, ranked on the
same Texas scale; distributor SAIDI always wins when present; the basis (distributor vs county proxy) is stored and shown with its source.
(2b) Score terms are anchored absolute scales, not percentiles (user decision: percentiles discarded magnitude, e.g. every Austin Energy
home's 181.98 SAIDI minutes ranked 0): each term = real value / anchor, capped at 1, anchors = 90th percentile of the real distribution
(outage: Texas utilities' EIA-861 SAIDI; medical need: Texas ZIPs; age 65+ and backup intent: Travis block groups; electric heat: the share
itself), stored with sources; the breakdown shows the real value and its anchor.
(3) Top signals = the signals where this home stands out: rank by w * (term - county median of that term), positive only; near-constant
terms (flood) are never a 'top signal' but still count in the score; home-level signals are preferred when tied.
Recreate dependents (mv_gate_counts, mv_gate_counts_by_market, api functions incl. api.home_score_breakdown) with the same terms;
refresh via core.refresh_all_scores() (add any new mv there); keep every request-time query an index lookup.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Homes in the same block group no longer all tie: page 1 of the county ranking has >= 10 distinct scores
- [ ] No single signal is a top signal for > 60% of ranked homes at equal weights; flood is never listed as a top signal
- [ ] Austin Energy homes show a non-zero outage percentile vs Texas distributors, with source
- [ ] Oncor homes get an outage term from the EAGLE-I county proxy, labelled as a county average with its source
- [ ] Permit-based signals are null (with reason) outside the City of Austin permit area, never false
- [ ] Contributions still sum to the score (±0.001); every request-time query < 1 s
- [ ] No score term is a percentile; Austin Energy homes get a non-zero outage term from their real minutes
